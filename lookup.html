02 — Sample only

Look up one address in the Palm Beach sample.

This search runs in your browser. It uses 9,447 Palm Beach rows from test/fixtures/palm-beach-locations.json: license, location address, licensee name, and business name. It is not the live DBPR extract, and it does not send what you type.

Try 3051 S Ocean Blvd, Boca Raton, the name BOCA REEF ASSN INC, or license 6728. An exact license wins over reading those digits as a zip.

The results will list every match in the sample. A blank business name says not in DBPR record. Class, status, install year, and servicer say not in this sample, except on license 6728, where those fields are transcribed from the recorded popup and the servicer is labeled per DBPR record.

What this sample leaves out

Miami-Dade and Broward coverage is partial, and those county lists are not in this file. City of Miami and Miami Beach run their own programs. A miss here does not mean the building has no elevators.

The sample file has no data date of its own. License 6728’s extra fields use the popup dated Oct 25, 2026, 6:48 AM ET.
