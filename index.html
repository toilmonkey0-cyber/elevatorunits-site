Partial county coverage

Miami-Dade and Broward coverage is partial. Miami-Dade uses the Office of Elevator Safety list, data as of Oct 28, 2026. Broward uses the Elevator Section release, data as of Oct 2026. Owner and install year on those rows say Not in county record. The DLM flag says Unknown, install year not in county record.

City of Miami and Miami Beach run their own programs. ElevatorUnits does not have their unit lists yet. The panel says: "This jurisdiction runs its own elevator program, so state data is not available here." A miss does not mean the building has no elevators.

The 2029 deadline

Florida rule 61C-5.001 adopts ASME A17.3-2020. Section 3.10.12 compliance was required from August 1, 2025. Enforcement is not until August 1, 2029.
