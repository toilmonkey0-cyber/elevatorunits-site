04 — Door lock monitoring

Enforcement is August 1, 2029.

Florida rule 61C-5.001 adopts ASME A17.3-2020. The extension’s flag uses the wording below. It does not say a door lock monitor is installed.

The flag

DLM check: ASME A17.3-2020 §3.10.12 compliance required from August 1, 2025, enforcement not until August 1, 2029 — verify DLM status, do not assume it is installed.

Not code advice. Confirm with a licensed elevator contractor and DBPR.

Who gets the flag

Only passenger traction (01) and passenger hydraulic (02) units with status 20, 21, or 45. Escalators, LULA, inclined, and other classes get no flag until coverage of the section is confirmed. Units that look like private residences get no flag.

Which classes §3.10.12 covers is not confirmed. The ASME text is paid and not public. DBPR says the rule applies to elevators, and Part X, private residence elevators, is excluded from adoption. The flag stays inside the limits above.

Tiers use install year only. DBPR has no modernization date. Higher priority means installed before 2000, or the year is unknown or impossible. Check activation means installed in 2000 or later. The 2000 cutoff is a guess that needs checking with contractors.

DBPR’s rule history lists the amendment of May 25, 2026. The compliance date in §3.10.12 is August 1, 2025. The enforcement date is August 1, 2029. Those are the dates in the rule text ElevatorUnits checked. This page does not add others.
